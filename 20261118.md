OK! Tried it out today.

Some improvement here. I messed about with cutting straight lines and found it cuts 3mm steel best at 300 mm/min with 2 second pierce,
and 5mm at 150 mm/min with 3 second pierce. This is still slower than I expect it should be!

Since switching to the new torch I have had no random disconnections at start of cutting at all so don't think this torch cable needs
any extra shielding, also no blown fuses. Clamp meter said it was drawing about 24 amps during cutting.

And I have cut out a heart from 5mm steel! This is my best 5mm part yet. The only previous part that completed at all was the picture
frame, and that involved 3 restarts due to tripping the breaker mid-cut. No problems this time, I just set it going and it completed
the cut. The bevel is *better* than before but still not ideal. Also I didn't need to use the grinder to cut this part out of the
plate.

So we are going in the right direction but I still think more is possible. I think the torch hanging off one side of the clamp means
the clamp is twisting and this is causing the bevel. So I may want adjustable tilt in the clamp after all. I also want to try a smaller
nozzle to see if it makes a tighter jet which will cut more efficiently.

This nozzle seems to be about 1.5mm, on the basis that I can fit a 1.4mm drill through it but not a 1.5mm one.

I've ordered a set of 1.1mm nozzles.

If the front leaf spring mount is all the way at the front of the chassis then the link
at the rear leaf spring mount interferes with the cross member. I don't mind making the
car smaller, and a shorter wheelbase is beneficial for steering, so probably just mount
the spring further back, potentially trim off excess chassis rail later.

Making this change in CAD moved it from a 1.81m wheelbase to 1.74m. According to Martin's
measurements of the John Haynes 750 special it has a 2.06m wheelbase and 1.0m front track
and 1.1m rear track.

My rear track comes to about 1.2m I think, so my car is shorter and wider.

# 2026-08-14

## Tractor weight distribution

We've fitted the ~10kg concrete block just behind the front axle, although I wouldn't say it was a massive success.

If you hadn't told me it was there I don't think I would have noticed, I would not say it has changed the handling perceptibly, which is quite disappointing.

Maybe the real problem is that the sack truck wheels have hardly any grip. I am tempted to try inflating them a bit more as they are a bit low on pressure, maybe being low
on pressure means they don't really have a preference for which direction they travel, seems unlikely.

I tried with both one-wheel-drive and two-wheel-drive, and in both cases it performed broadly the same as it did without the extra weight.

Moving the seat forwards has a much bigger effect than adding the concrete weight.

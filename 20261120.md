## Scale model of solar system

If we were to put a scale model of the solar system along the fence in the garden, where would things end up?

We have 128m if starting at the road and ending at the bottom of the garden or 98m if only the grass, or 104m
if starting at the back of the house, or 80m if starting at the grass and ending at the solar panels.

I think the 80m version is more natural.

So where would things be?

Neptune is the furthest, average distance about 30.2AU, 80m, so 0.378 AU/metre.

So:

 * Sun: 0AU, 0m
 * Mercury: 0.45AU, 1.19m
 * Venus: 0.72AU, 1.90m
 * Earth: 1AU, 2.65m
 * Mars: 1.53AU, 4.05m
 * (Asteroid belt: 2.3-3.3AU, 6.1-8.7m)
 * Jupiter: 5.2AU, 13.8m
 * Saturn: 9.6AU, 25.4m
 * Uranus: 18.7AU, 49.5m
 * Neptune: 30.2AU, 80m

Separately we can ask how large they should be, but I expect that will have to be a different scale otherwise
they will be mostly invisible.

Unfortunately I think having Mercury,Venus,Earth all in the first 3 metres means at least some of them will have to
be inside bushes.

There is a big bush from about 1.7 to 4.8 metres, which covers Venus, Earth, and Mars.

And annoyingly Saturn ends up in this bush if starting from the road and ending at the solar panels.

Starting from the road and ending at the very bottom looks like it would work? Not so keen on having to walk around
the back of the garage to see the majority of it though.

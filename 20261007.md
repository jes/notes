## Mower oil change

I changed the oil & filter on the mower today, it has done about 113 hours. Apparently John
Deere say you should do it every 50 hours, or yearly. Yearly seems unnecessary given the amount
of use it gets, so I think 50 hours is probably fine.

The oil that came out looked brand new. I've done about 14 hours since owning it, and unsure when
it was serviced before I got it.

I used a random spare oil filter that I had lying around that happened to fit, rather than buying a new one.

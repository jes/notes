Let's try moving 100mm back and forth but from different starting positions.

OK I found out what is wrong, it is riding over the flange on the pulley: https://img.incoherency.co.uk/6767

This changes the effective diameter so changes the distance it travels per revolution of the motor.

Currently I just have one bearing for each idler, but really wants 2 to stop it rocking. So I think redesign the idler
to have 2 bearings, and redesign the motor pulley to be longer and maybe have a larger diameter flange.

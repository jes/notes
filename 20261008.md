## VFD error

It would sometimes spin up OK and sometimes buzz for a second and then show E0007. ChatGPT
pointed out I had this same error in [2024-07-26](20240726.md), it means output over-current.

It turned out that the U phase wire had come detached in the connector that plugs into the motor,
probably badly soldered by me in the past. I soldered it back on and the problem was resolved.

## Lead weights

I made this: https://img.incoherency.co.uk/6646

The end mill was very good, it was a 4mm "DLC" coated single-flute end mill from Amazon, no particular name brand,
funny blue and yellow coating, cuts well and leaves a good finish. I used an air blast to clear the chips, I should
potentially rig something up to automate this instead of standing there using the compressor manually.

I then realised I don't have a convenient way to pour the molten lead so I have ordered a casting ladle from Amazon,

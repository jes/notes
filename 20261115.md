## Plasma cutter current measurement

Rather than buy a DC clamp meter, I think the plan is:

 * clip multimeter across both ends of the work return cable
 * pass a known 5A through the work return cable using the bench power supply
 * try same at different known currents just to check
 * V=IR, measure the voltage across the work return cable to work out its resistance
 * then put the work return cable back on the plasma cutter but leave multimeter clipped on
 * V=IR, measure the voltage across the work return cable to find the current

And then if it's broadly 20A or whatever I selected on the dial then we know there is enough current and poor
cutting performance is due to something else. And if there's not enough current then the plasma cutter is maybe defective?

Also would be good to measure the AC voltage the plasma cutter is getting during cutting. If the wiring is too resistant
then the AC voltage will drop too much, especially given the high current it is drawing, and that may exacerbate the
problem if it then tries to draw even more current to make up for the low voltage.

## 3d printing tungsten weights

I just woke up with maybe a good insight: would I have been better off printing these weights out of tungesten-filled filament or something?

Firstly let's look at density. I got codex to add https://incoherency.co.uk/jesref/material-densities.html

Lead is about 11.3 g/cm^3, a 75% by-weight tungsten-filled filament is 4 g/cm^3, so already it's only about 1/3 as good. That may be enough
to rule it out already.

As for costs: I already had the lead shot so we can call that 0, but I had to buy the pouring ladle which cost £10, and a pack of end mills
for machining the mould which cost £18 for 3.

I still have the end mills so you can amortise some of that cost, and I used up some of my lead stock so maybe that ought to be worth some
cost as well.

Prusa's tungsten-filled PETG is currently out of stock and is not showing a price.

OK, on second thoughts: forget it. The cheapest tungsten-filled filament I found is $500/kg, and remember at 3x the density of PLA it won't
go very far. Most of the sources are over $1000/kg.

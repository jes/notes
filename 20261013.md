## Dehumidifier drain

The other day I noticed that the water collection tray was full.

The drain hose was still installed so I couldn't really see why water was collecting in the tray. I observed it
for a few minutes and noticed that water was collecting at the top of the hose, and building up due to surface
tension, and then instead of breaking the surface tension and tumbling down the hose it would
spring a tiny leak at the top of the hose and drip into the tray! Unbelievable. I wouldn't believe it if I hadn't
seen it.

The Meaco advice is apparently to drill a hole in your hose to allow air to get in, which I did and thought it
had fixed it, but today again I noticed that the water collection tray was full.

This time I sprayed some WD40 on the inside of the hose and now the water slips down it nicely without surface
tension holding a big globule of water in place. Hopefully that's the end of the problem.

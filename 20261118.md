## Plasma cutter torch

I ordered a "SUSEMSE P-80" torch off Amazon:

https://img.incoherency.co.uk/6836

There is a review in German that says he used it on a Lidl plasma cutter, and a video demo of it working.

The air/negative connection screws on easily, the pilot arc return is a different connection but is
easy to connect to the Parkside one. You just pull the rubber insulation off the fitting and undo
the allen screw.

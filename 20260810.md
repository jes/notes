## Tractor trailer

I've been doing the green paint on the wooden panels. It is going very badly.

https://img.incoherency.co.uk/6665

I thought it would be fun and interesting to do it on the grass. How wrong I was! It is getting covered in bits of dead grass, terrible,
and I am having to squat/crouch in order to do it. I should have done it on the workbench in the garage, obviously.

The green paint is "racing green, gloss, non-drop" from "151 coatings". It seems to be a very good paint, covers
nicely, can't complain about the quality.

It's very runny in the can but very viscous once it comes out. It has a tendency to drip off the brush even after you
wipe it on the edge of the can, and it is very difficult to clean off your fingers.

Also I don't have any white spirit to clean the brushes. I have been leaving them submerged in water to stop them drying out (
does this do anything?) and reusing them the next day.

The best way I have found to clean the paint off my fingers is to spray Zoflora on my fingers: https://img.incoherency.co.uk/6666

Using a white microabrasive sponge is *not* a good idea, I have given myself some quite bad carpet-burn-type rashes on my
forearm.

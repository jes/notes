# 2026-09-23

## Cyclecar spindles

I put some M20x2.0 threads on the end of some pieces of 20mm EN8 bar:

https://img.incoherency.co.uk/6716

They then didn't *quite* fit through the wheel bearings so I have been polishing them down
with green "Scotchbrite" type material and they do fit now but still on the tight side. It may
loosen up enough when the spindles cool down, else I'll probably do more polishing.

## Plasma cutter

I've been trying to get this working this evening.

Problems are that it doesn't penetrate very fast, doesn't cut very fast, and leaves a lot of slag and a heavily parallelogram kerf.

Turning up the air pressure and removing a restrictive section of air hose seems to have *helped* with the slag but not necessarily
as good as it ought to be.

I can't find anything wrong either mechanically or in the nozzle that looks like it would cause a parallelogram kerf, it's about 20&deg;
away from vertical.

## Plasma CAM

We should be able to get an AI-generated CAM program for plasma cutting:

 * easy import from FreeCAD (project STL/3MF on to flat, plus DXF and SVG)
 * scale and mirror option
 * rotate/translate for manual nesting
 * tell it the size of your material and mark keep-out zones
 * auto-nest option (https://github.com/azmtkrdnv/sheetnest ? or from scratch)
 * auto lead in/out but option to tweak start/end point or lead distance per profile
 * "settings library" where you can have like kerf width, feed rate, pierce delay stored under names, say "3mm steel"
 * show the shapes & paths on a 2d view where "keep parts" are filled in slightly different to background colour, cut automatically goes on correct side and direction
 * output gcode for grbl with clear comments explaining what each section is, I find this useful when manually cutting parts out of FreeCAD's gcode

## Cyclecar uprights

Some more machining on these: https://img.incoherency.co.uk/6706

https://img.incoherency.co.uk/6707

The D-shaped pocket in the top is in aid of aligning the steering arm, if I put a D-shape
on the steering arm at the correct angle then when it drops into this piece to get welded
it will be at the correct angle automatically.

The 16mm hole in the middle is interpolated, and seems to be coming out a bit under-size, this is
potentially good as long as the bolt still goes through it because it means it is aligned more accurately.
And if the bolt won't go through it I can just enlarge it until it does.

And finished: https://img.incoherency.co.uk/6708

Needs a bit of deburring etc.

# 2026-09-24

## Cyclecar uprights

Off-axis holes drilled in each of these: https://img.incoherency.co.uk/6718

They're 8.5 degrees off axis, I held the uprights in the vice on top of the 3d-printed block
seen at left of pic.

As a test, I also tightened up a bolt across the inner races of the wheel bearings in one of the wheels,
just to check that it would tighten up across the spacer and not bind up, and unfortunately it bound up.

It is possible that I need to put some sort of sleeve inside to make sure the spacer is properly
aligned with the bearings, but so far not looking good. Also possible that I may just need to tap
the bearings in a bit further.

Update: I tapped the bearings in further and as if by magic the inner races are now tight up against the
spacer and still turn freely! Great success.

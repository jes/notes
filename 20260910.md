## Cyclecar

I've welded on the leaf spring mounts: https://img.incoherency.co.uk/6682

And I'm pretty sure this gives enough clearance between the spring and the chassis rail for the axle to be mounted
on top of the spring instead of underneath, which then means the uprights for the steering don't need to come up
so far.

And copied into CAD: https://img.incoherency.co.uk/6683

Update: looking at the picture in CAD it is clear that the shackle angle is much too shallow. I think the shackle
gets fully horizontal long before the spring is flattened out, probably I need a longer shackle. Probably I should
have welded the mounts further back.

I think the shackles want to be 80mm long instead of 60mm long.

## Keyboard Glockenspiel

Easy solution to the black keys going down too far: put 2 o-rings on the guide pin: https://img.incoherency.co.uk/6671

The reason it's a problem is because it means if you hold the key down then the hammer remains in contact with the tone bar,
acting as a damper.

Next is that some of the black keys ever so slightly interfere with the overlapping white keys, which keeps them stuck down at full
press. I think the solution will be to cut some of the white key plastic away to create clearance.

And there is still a bit of a rattley sound when the key is released, I wonder if I can do anything about that.

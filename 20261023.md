For attaching plastic parts to 1.6mm thickness steel I am going to use the sheet metal roofing screws. They want a 6.5mm
clearance hole.

Actually instead of a 3d-printed rack-and-pinion I might just use GT2 belt as a rack, with like a "omega drive" for it.
That way the problem of reducing backlash on the rack is reduced to tensioning the belt, plus I already have belt & reduces
time/material in 3d printing.

I have pieces of GT2 belt 65cm long and 109cm long. Will need to print pulleys to go on the Leadshine motors.

Y axis will be omega drive, just needs the 1 pulley on the motor plus a couple of idler bearings.

X axis will be a looped belt, needs 1 pulley on the motor plus an idler pulley at the other end.

Tensioning in both cases can just be a part with a bolt that pushes into the belt to stretch it, in a place where it's not going to have
to run over the pulleys.

I generated a 30-tooth GT2 pulley with the TimingGear macro from the addon manager.

I have a pair of bearings labelled 6002RS to use as idlers on the omega drive. And I have 12x 6701 bearings to use
for carriage wheels.
